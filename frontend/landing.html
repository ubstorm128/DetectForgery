SIH Problem Statement 26188 · Ministry of Home Affairs · SSB

Catching forged travel documents before they cross the line.

An explainable screening system for passports and visas — validating MRZ checksums, cross-field consistency, and photo tampering, and flagging multi-identity fraud against a watchlist. Built for checkpoint conditions: fast, offline-capable, and transparent about every decision it makes.

The problem

Border checkpoints process thousands of documents a day — verification hasn't kept up.

Current checks rely on human inspection and basic database lookups. That leaves gaps forgers already exploit.

01

Fake passports, visas, and altered photographs pass basic visual inspection.

02

Modified dates of birth and tampered visa stamps go unnoticed under time pressure.

03

The same person uses multiple identities across expired or blacklisted documents.

Our approach

Deterministic checks first. Machine learning only where it earns its place.

Instead of a black-box fraud score, every flag names exactly what failed — so an officer can verify the reasoning, not just trust a number.

01
MRZ checksum validation
Runs the actual ICAO 9303 check-digit algorithm on the machine-readable zone — a deterministic, verifiable test, not a guess.
02
Cross-field consistency
Confirms date of birth matches across zones, visa dates fall within passport validity, and stamp sequences are chronologically possible.
03
Photo tamper detection
Error-level analysis surfaces splicing and recompression artifacts around the photo region.
04
Multi-identity matching
Face embeddings compared against a watchlist to catch one person using several identities.
05
Offline-first inference
Runs on-device — checkpoint connectivity doesn't gate a decision.
